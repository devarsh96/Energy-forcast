    About this report

    • Built from IESO's hourly generator output by fuel for Ontario transmission-connected generators. Imports, exports and embedded generation are not included.
    • Intensity uses IPCC AR5 lifecycle median emission factors (g CO₂e/kWh): nuclear 12, gas 490, hydro 24, wind 11, solar 48, biofuel 230. Storage is not counted.
    • Times are Toronto local time. A month in progress covers the hours reported so far.
