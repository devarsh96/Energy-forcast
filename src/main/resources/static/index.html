              Prices

                • The plan comparison and the "Cheapest charge" box use your EV settings from the Savings Calculator.
                • Cheapest = lowest cost. Cleanest = lowest kg CO₂e. Each charge starts at the top of an hour and runs at full charger power, allowing about 10% charging loss.
                • Full-bill estimate = (price + delivery) × (1 + HST − Ontario Electricity Rebate). Delivery charges vary by utility, so check your bill for your number.

                Emission factors: IPCC AR5 Working Group III (2014), Annex III, lifecycle median values. Data: IESO public reports (GenOutputCapability, Adequacy3, Ontario Zonal Price); OEB Regulated Price Plan prices.
