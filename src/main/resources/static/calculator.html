How this is worked out

  • EV planner: the charge starts at the top of an hour and runs at full charger power until done. Energy from the wall = battery × (target − now) ÷ 90%, allowing about 10% charging loss.
  • Cheapest = lowest cost on your plan. Cleanest = lowest kg CO₂e. Best balance weighs cost and CO₂ equally. When they pick the same time they are shown as one card.
  • Household tasks: the best time is the run of consecutive hours, within the next 24, with the lowest average forecast intensity. "Right now" uses the latest IESO hour, then the forecast.
  • Costs use the rate plan picked in the top bar (Ultra-Low Overnight unless you changed it), electricity price only unless you turned on the full-bill estimate there.
  • The driving comparison uses about 190 g CO₂ per km for an average gasoline car (a rough assumption, for scale only).
